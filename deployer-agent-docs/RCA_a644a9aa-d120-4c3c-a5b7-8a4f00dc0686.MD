### Which date field did the policy match on?

- The rule's validity window is exactly one day: `valid_from = valid_to = 2026-10-12`.

### Why the edit still affected the already-uploaded policy

Commission is resolved at **statement-generation time** against whatever rule matches. The statement `CS-202610-0001` was generated 2026-10-13T01:34:59Z — right after the rule was versioned (01:33:57) and before the new version's last edit (01:36:16) — and it covers the whole month (10-01…10-31), so it re-evaluated this 10-12 policy and picked up the TATA AIG payable rule at Net 72% → payable 22356. Because nothing is locked on the policy and `apply_to_past=false` does not prevent inclusion in a fresh monthly statement, regenerating the statement re-applies the current rule to the "past" policy. The one-day validity did not shield the policy because the window matched its upload date (10-12), not its risk start date (10-13).
